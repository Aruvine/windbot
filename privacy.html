Who We Are

Wind Bot ("the Bot", "we", "our") is a Discord bot that runs a partner advertising network: servers that add the Bot exchange advertisements with each other automatically, by hand using partner tokens, or through paid ads. It also sells an optional Premium upgrade, and it is run with the help of a small staff team.

The Bot is operated by its developer ("Developer"). For contact information, see Section 13.

💡
The Developer also runs a Minecraft community server of their own, where Wind Bot provides extra community features such as a trading shop and games. Those features run only in that one server, they involve no real-world money, and everything in this policy applies to them in the same way.

Messages the Bot Reads

Wind Bot does not collect or store general chat. It reads the content of messages only in the following places, and only to run the feature named:

  • Your configured ad channel: to find and send your server's partner ad.
  • Your configured partner channel: to notice when a partner's ad is deleted, which is what a strike is for.
  • Channels where a link is posted: to deduplicate partner links and to check that an ad's invite really points at the server advertising it.
  • Staff ticket channels: a transcript is saved to the staff log when the ticket closes, because closing it deletes the channel.
  • Direct messages to the bot and relay conversations: when you are replying to a staff application, answering a message from the team, or being asked why your server left.
  • /wave editing sessions: while you are writing or editing a saved wave.
⚠️
To know which of these places a new message belongs to, the bot checks where each message was sent. Messages anywhere else are ignored and not stored.

Why We Collect It

All data collection is tied to a specific feature:

  • Guild, channel & role IDs and partner settings: to decide who your server is matched with and to post partner ads in the right place with the right ping.
  • Ad content and partner links: your ad is the thing being exchanged, so it has to be read to be sent. Links are compared so the same server is not advertised to you twice.
  • Ad deliveries and member counts: to work out whose ads actually bring members in, which is what the matching improves on. Only public, server-level member counts are used. There is no per-user tracking and no click attribution.
  • Partnership records and tokens: to enforce cooldowns so the same two servers are not paired repeatedly, and to run the token economy.
  • Strikes and blacklists: a received partner ad has to stay up, or the exchange is one-sided. Strikes record who took one down and whose it was, so a strike can be taken back when the deletion turns out to have been justified.
  • User IDs: to know who may change a server's settings, and who to contact about it.
  • Premium and purchase records: to deliver what was bought, apply it to the right server, and handle refunds, chargebacks and disputes.
  • Staff records: to run applications fairly, to know what staff have asked for and what was decided, and to pay referral commission accurately.
  • Relayed messages: so a conversation the bot carries has a record, and so one person cannot flood another through it.
  • Win-back contacts: to ask an owner why their server left and whether they would come back.

Premium Purchases & Payment Data

Premium is sold through Tebex, an external payment provider. Your payment is made on Tebex's checkout, not to the Bot.

✅
Wind Bot never sees, receives, or stores your payment card details, bank details, or billing address. Those are handled entirely by Tebex and its own payment processors, under Tebex's privacy policy.

When a payment completes, Tebex sends the Bot a notification about the order. From it the Bot stores:

  • The order's ID at Tebex, and the package bought.
  • The amount and currency.
  • The name on the order, and your Discord user ID when Tebex knows it.
  • The server ID you gave at checkout, so Premium reaches the right server.
  • The referral ID, if a staff member's link was used, so their commission can be worked out.
  • Whether the order was granted, held, refunded or ignored, and any note a person added.
  • The notification itself, as sent. It is kept whole because an order that goes wrong, such as one where the server ID was mistyped, can only be put right by looking at exactly what arrived.

The same applies to subscription events: a renewal, a cancellation, a refund or a chargeback each arrive the same way and are recorded the same way.

Messages Carried Between People

Staff never message you from their own accounts. When a staff member needs to speak to somebody outside the team, such as an applicant or the owner of a server that removed the Bot, the Bot carries the conversation between them.

  • You are always told that you are speaking to the Wind Bot team, and never see the staff member's personal account.
  • Every message either way is stored, with who wrote it and when. That record is what staff and the owner can review if a conversation is disputed.
  • Each conversation is claimed by one staff member, so you are not passed around.
  • You can end the conversation with a button at any time. Nothing more will reach you through the Bot afterwards.

Data Storage & Security

Data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database, and the Bot is hosted on Railway. Access to the database is restricted to the Developer via secret credentials that are never exposed publicly.

  • The database is not publicly accessible, and every table has Row Level Security enabled with no public policies.
  • Access is protected by provider access controls on top of that.
  • Real-world payments are handled entirely by Tebex. No card or bank details ever reach the Bot or its database.
✅
Your data is used only to operate Wind Bot's features. It is never sold or shared for advertising.

Data Retention

  • Guild configuration: retained while the Bot remains in your server, and kept afterwards so your settings are still there if you add it back. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 10).
  • Server contact details and win-back entries: kept after the Bot is removed from a server, so the owner can still be reached and asked why.
  • Partner links: daily records are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Ad delivery records: kept so the performance statistics stay meaningful over time.
  • Strikes: kept, including ones that were taken back, because the fact that a strike was reversed is part of the record.
  • Blacklist entries: kept until the server is removed from the blacklist.
  • Purchase and Premium records: kept for as long as needed to handle refunds, chargebacks and accounting, and are not deleted on request while a dispute or a legal obligation to keep them stands.
  • Staff records, royalties and tag changes: kept as the team's history.
  • Ticket transcripts and relayed messages: kept as the record of what was said.
  • Error logs: only the most recent 200 are kept; older entries are deleted automatically.

Third Parties

Wind Bot relies on the following third-party services to function, and does not share your data with anyone else:

  • Discord (discord.com): required for the Bot to operate at all. Discord's own privacy policy applies to data held by Discord.
  • Supabase: hosts the database where the data described above is stored.
  • Railway: hosts the Bot's application.
  • Tebex (tebex.io): processes Premium payments and sends the Bot the order notifications described in Section 5. Tebex is the seller of record for those purchases.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website. GitHub may log requests to it, such as your IP address, as part of serving the page.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com): this website loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google when you open these pages. This applies to the website only, not to the Bot.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party for advertising or commercial purposes.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: request a summary of what data is stored for your server or user ID.
  • Deletion: request that data associated with your server or user ID be deleted. Records we are required to keep, such as purchase and refund records, and records needed to resolve an open dispute or to prevent fraud, may be kept.
  • Correction: update stored configuration yourself with the Bot's slash commands, such as /config.
  • Objection: ask that a specific optional record, such as a win-back entry, be removed. You can also end a relayed conversation at any time with the button on it.

To exercise these rights, contact us via Section 13.

Children's Privacy

Wind Bot is not directed at children under the age of 13, and Discord's own Terms of Service require users to be at least 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, contact us and it will be deleted.

Premium is a paid product. You must be 18 or older, or have the permission of a parent or guardian who holds the payment method, to buy it. See the Terms of Service for the purchase terms.
