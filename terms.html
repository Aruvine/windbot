The Partner Network

The partner network exchanges server advertisements between servers where Wind Bot is configured. By setting it up (via /config), you agree to the following:

  • The exchange is mutual. Your server's ad, which is the most recent message in your configured ad channel, is sent to other participating servers, and their ads are posted in your partner channel. Taking a partner's ad down while yours stays up is not allowed, and is what strikes exist for.
  • Your ad is copied to other servers. Once it has been posted elsewhere, it is out of the Developer's hands. The Bot cannot recall an ad that has already been delivered.
  • Your ad content must comply with Discord's Community Guidelines and these Terms. Content that is NSFW, promotes harmful communities, or violates Discord's policies is strictly prohibited.
  • Your ad must advertise your own server and must carry a working invite to it.
  • Matching is not guaranteed. Who you are paired with, how often, and whether you are paired at all depend on your tag, your member range, your cooldown, who else is available, and how your ad has performed. No particular number of partnerships is promised, on any tier.
  • Pings are your responsibility. You choose whether partner ads ping a role in your server, and which role. You are responsible for that role's membership and for what your members are pinged for.
  • The Developer may remove any server from the network at any time, for any reason, including policy violations.

Strikes, Blacklisting & Appeals

  • Deleting a partner ad you received earns your server a strike. Three strikes blacklist your server from the network automatically.
  • A strike is taken back if the server whose ad you deleted is itself blacklisted within three days, because deleting a bad server's ad was the right call. This does not apply when that server was blacklisted for deleting ads of its own: its ad was honest, so taking it down was not.
  • The Developer may blacklist a server by hand, with or without strikes, for anything in Section 5.
  • A blacklisted server receives no partner ads and sends none, and cannot be partnered even manually.
  • If a blacklist is lifted, servers blacklisted solely because of that server's ad are restored and the matching strike is removed.
  • To appeal a strike or a blacklist, contact the Developer or open a ticket with the staff team. Appeals are decided at the Developer's discretion and that decision is final.
